# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct: Neurodiversity & Inclusion Standard

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in DxSkills a harassment-free, accessible, and empowering experience for everyone, regardless of neurodiversity status, cognitive style, age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, level of experience, education, or socio-economic status.

We commit to fostering an open, welcoming, diverse, and respectful environment specifically calibrated for non-linear, spatial, and dyslexic thinkers.

---

## Neurodivergent-First Standards

Our community actively practices these positive behaviors:

1. **Zero Spelling & Grammar Pedantry:** We never criticize, mock, or call out spelling, typo, or grammatical errors in issues, pull requests, or discussions. Focus entirely on the substantive idea, architecture, or insight.
2. **Direct, Grounded Communication:** We encourage directness and clarity over bureaucratic diplomacy or formal posturing.
3. **Patience with Non-Linear Input:** We value unstructured brain dumps, bullet fragments, and voice-to-text transcripts equally with polished prose.
4. **Constructive Scaffolding:** When giving feedback, offer constructive structural suggestions and visual diagrams rather than demands for linear conformity.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Spelling-shaming, grammar-policing, or condescending remarks regarding a contributor's communication mechanics.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.

---

## Enforcement & Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

### Reporting
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via the GitHub repository issue tracker or directly to Dr. Moulay Anwar Sounny-Slitine. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
