# Cognitive Intake Template: Meeting Transcript Digest
# Used by: dx-read (/dx read meeting)

## Operational Objective
Decomposes 45 to 60 minute rambling meeting transcripts (Zoom, Teams, Otter.ai) into a crisp, decision-oriented executive brief.

---

## Standard Output Format

```markdown
# Meeting Executive Digest: [Meeting Subject]

> **Date / Time:** [Timestamp] | **Participants:** [Names / Roles]
> **BLUF:** [1-2 sentences summarizing the single most important decision or roadblock]

---

## 1. Concrete Decisions Made
- **Decision 1:** [Exact agreed-upon direction and rationale]
- **Decision 2:** [Technical or organizational choice made]

## 2. Action Items & Commitments
| Action Item | Assignee | Target Deadline | Blocker / Dependency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## 3. Discarded Options & Open Debates
- **Rejected Path:** [Option discussed and why consensus went against it]
- **Open Issue:** [Topic requiring follow-up before next sync]
```
