# Cognitive Intake Template: Policy Memo Reframe
# Used by: dx-read (/dx read policy)

## Operational Objective
Converts dense, convoluted legal, HR, or compliance policy documents into unambiguous visual guidelines and rule matrices.

---

## Standard Output Format

```markdown
# Policy Breakdown: [Policy Name / Guideline]

> **Issuing Body:** [Department / Agency] | **Effective Date:** [Date]
> **BLUF:** [2 sentences explaining who is affected, what changes, and what must be done]

---

## 1. Allowed vs. Prohibited Matrix
| Permitted Activities | Prohibited Actions | Requires Prior Approval |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ... | ... | ... |

## 2. Immediate Compliance Steps
1. **Action 1:** [Step required with deadline]
2. **Action 2:** [Documentation submission requirements]

## 3. Exceptions & Escalation Path
- **Who to contact:** [Department or contact point]
- **Appeals process:** [Direct timeline and requirements]
```
