# Socratic Interview Template: Research Grant Proposal
# Used by: dx-interview (/dx ask grant)

## Phase 1: Interactive Question Wave
Ask these 4 targeted questions to the researcher. Instruct them to answer in quick bullet points or rough speech dictation:

1. **The Critical Tension:** What specific unresolved problem or empirical gap exists in the literature today that makes this research urgent?
2. **The Methodology & Innovation:** What novel method, dataset, or computational architecture will you use to solve this?
3. **The Measurable Deliverables:** At the end of the grant period, what tangible outcomes will exist (e.g., open-source models, peer-reviewed articles, benchmarks)?
4. **The Strategic Alignment:** Which specific funding priority or call topic does this proposal directly address?

---

## Phase 2: Compiler Synthesis Template
Once the researcher replies, compile their answers into this structured format:

```markdown
# [Project Title: Bold and Direct]

> **Executive Summary:** [2-sentence high-impact abstract]
> **Total Duration:** [Months] | **Target Call:** [Agency / Program]

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Significance
- **The Empirical Gap:** [Synthesized from Question 1]
- **Societal / Scientific Impact:** [Long-term transformative outcome]

## 2. Research Methodology & Work Packages
| Work Package | Title | Primary Objective | Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WP1** | Data Ingestion & Harmonization | Clean, validate, and index datasets | Benchmark corpus |
| **WP2** | Model Architecture & Training | Develop and evaluate novel models | Pre-trained weights |
| **WP3** | Validation & Dissemination | Peer review, workshops, and open release | Published papers |

## 3. Measurable Milestones & Timeline
- **Milestone 1 (Month 3):** [Core data pipeline operational]
- **Milestone 2 (Month 6):** [Initial baseline evaluation published]
- **Milestone 3 (Month 12):** [Final dissemination and open-source release]
```
