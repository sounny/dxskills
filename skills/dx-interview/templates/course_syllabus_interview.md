# Socratic Interview Template: Academic Course Syllabus
# Used by: dx-interview (/dx ask syllabus)

## Phase 1: Interactive Question Wave
Ask these 4 targeted questions to the instructor:

1. **Course Identity:** What is the course title, audience level (undergrad, masters, executive), and primary learning outcome?
2. **Pedagogical Arc:** What are the 3 major thematic modules the students will journey through across the semester?
3. **Assessment Philosophy:** How will students prove mastery (e.g., weekly coding labs, final term project, oral presentations)?
4. **Prerequisites & Tools:** What software, programming languages, or prior knowledge are required on day one?

---
