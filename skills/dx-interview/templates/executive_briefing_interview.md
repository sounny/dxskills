# Socratic Interview Template: Executive Briefing
# Used by: dx-interview (/dx ask brief)

## Phase 1: Interactive Question Wave
Ask these 3 direct questions to the leader:

1. **The Executive Decision:** What specific approval, budget allocation, or policy sign-off is needed from leadership right now?
2. **The Urgency & Stakes:** Why must this decision be made today rather than next quarter? What is the cost of delay?
3. **The Core Trade-Offs:** What are the 2 competing options and why is your recommended path superior?

---

## Phase 2: Compiler Synthesis Template
Compile the spoken answers into this 1-page executive brief:

```markdown
# Executive Briefing: [Decision Title]

> **Decision Requested:** [1-sentence explicit ask from Question 1]
> **Deadline for Decision:** [Date] | **Financial / Strategic Impact:** [Metric]

---

## 1. Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)
[Concise synthesis of stakes, recommended action, and urgency from Questions 1 and 2]

## 2. Decision Matrix & Option Comparison
| Metric / Criteria | Option A: [Recommended] | Option B: [Status Quo / Alt] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Capital Investment** | [Cost] | [Cost] |
| **Time to Value** | [Timeline] | [Timeline] |
| **Operational Risk** | [Managed risks] | [Unmitigated risk / Cost of inaction] |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed Immediately** | Reject |

## 3. Implementation Steps (Post-Approval)
1. **Week 1:** Finalize legal and security review.
2. **Week 2:** Execute vendor contracts and commence onboarding.
3. **Week 4:** Milestone 1 review and initial metric reporting.
```
