# Cognitive Dump Template: Journal Editor Cover Letter
# Used by: dx-dump (/dx dump coverletter)

## Operational Objective
Converts scattered thoughts and paper abstracts into an authoritative, high-signal journal editor cover letter that highlights scholarly novelty and fit within 30 seconds of reading.

---
