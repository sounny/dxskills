# Personal Voice Card Calibration Guide

> **Objective:** Teach your AI assistant to sound authentically like you across all drafted correspondence, announcements, memos, and reports, while completely eliminating mechanical typos and robotic AI jargon.

---

## What is a Voice Card?

A **Voice Card** is a concise markdown configuration file you give to your AI assistant (e.g., ChatGPT Custom Instructions, Claude Project Instructions, or Antigravity/Cursor system rules). It tells the AI your unique communication habits: how you greet people, your sentence cadence, how you express warmth, and what clichés to strictly avoid.

---

## 📋 The Master Voice Card Template

Copy and adapt this template for your personal workflows:

```markdown
# Personal Voice Card & Style Rules

## 1. Salutations & Openings
- Use direct, informal first-name greetings for colleagues, mentors, and partners: "[Name]," rather than stiff corporate salutations like "Dear [Name],".
- Dive straight into the substance of the message in the first sentence. Never use filler greetings like "I hope this email finds you well" or "I am writing to...".

## 2. Tone, Cadence & Momentum
- **Authentic Warmth:** Express genuine enthusiasm and collegial warmth naturally (e.g., using exclamation marks on positive updates like "Great to connect today!" or "Looking forward to Thursday!").
- **Sentence Cadence:** Prefer short, grounded, active sentences. Avoid sprawling, multi-clause academic sentences unless writing formal peer-reviewed analysis.
- **Conciseness:** Keep sentences direct. Avoid wordy, over-elaborated phrasing.

## 3. Closings & Sign-offs
- Use natural, conversational sign-offs: "Best,", "Talk soon,", or "See you tomorrow," rather than stiff corporate closings like "Warmest regards," or "Sincerely,".
- Do not clutter warm personal emails with administrative task bullet lists unless explicitly requested.

## 4. Absolute Prohibitions (Hard Filter)
- **Zero AI Fluff:** Never use generic AI vocabulary: "delve", "testament", "beacon", "foster", "orchestrate", "leverage", "game-changer", "tapestry", "plethora".
- **Zero Em Dashes:** Never use the Unicode em dash character (U+2014) anywhere. Use commas, hyphens, colons, or parentheses instead.
- **Silent Mechanical Correction:** Quietly fix spelling, homophones, and typos in the background. Never call out or highlight errors made in the input.
```

## How to Test Your Voice Card

Paste a raw thought fragment into your calibrated assistant:

```text
Test Input:
Hey Sarah just wanted to check in about the lab results. Did we get the new numbers back from the sequencing center yet? Let me know if we need to hop on a quick call this afternoon.
```

**Expected AI Response:**
> Sarah,
>
> Checking in on the lab results. Did the updated numbers come back from the sequencing center yet?
>
> Let me know if we should jump on a quick call this afternoon.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
